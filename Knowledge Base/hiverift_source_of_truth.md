## 7-Phase Sales Process (SOP)

**Phase 1 – Lead Capture**
Incoming leads via website form, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or referral. SDR logs into CRM within 5 minutes.

**Phase 2 – Initial Qualification**
SDR conducts a 10-minute discovery call to assess budget, timeline, and need. Qualify using BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline).

**Phase 3 – Needs Analysis**
BDE schedules a detailed 30-minute call. Document client requirements, pain points, and success metrics.

**Phase 4 – Proposal Creation**
BDE drafts a custom proposal within 24 hours. Proposal includes scope, timeline, pricing, and deliverables.

**Phase 5 – Proposal Presentation**
BDE presents proposal to the client via video call. Address objections and refine scope if needed.

**Phase 6 – Negotiation & Closure**
Handle pricing negotiations within approved discount bands (max 15% discount). Obtain signed agreement and 50% advance payment.

**Phase 7 – Handover & Onboarding**
Sales team briefs delivery team. Client onboarding call scheduled within 48 hours of payment.

## Team Roles

### SDR (Sales Development Representative)
- First point of contact for all inbound leads
- Responsible for lead qualification (Phases 1–2)
- Logs all interactions into CRM
- Escalates qualified leads to BDE within 1 hour
- KPI: 20 qualified leads per week

### BDE (Business Development Executive)
- Handles qualified leads from SDR
- Responsible for Phases 3–7
- Owns proposal creation and deal closure
- Maintains client relationship post-closure until handover
- KPI: 5 closed deals per month, ₹5L monthly revenue target
